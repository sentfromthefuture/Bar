Cost is the supplier price plus any extra cost per unit (breakages, fees). Leave Own % empty to use the default. Rent and wages are not included here; use the Profit target tab for those.

Markups worth testing

- Mainstream beers: 25–40%
- Premium beers and ciders: 30–50%
- Soft drinks and water: 40–70%
- Standard spirits by the bottle: 25–40%
- Premium spirits and champagne: 20–35%
- Wine: 30–50%
- Shisha supplies: 30–50%

These are starting points to test, not verified Zambian averages. Compare with nearby bars before you commit.
